02 · Service

NBA intelligence as an integration layer.

DelQuant delivers structured NBA projections, player context, historical replay, roster intelligence, and decision tools through a FastAPI JSON service. Use it as your primary projections provider or alongside an existing feed when you need a deeper model layer.

  • Nightly refresh with optional morning refresh
  • Pre-game projections and scheduled delivery
  • JSON responses designed for product, analyst, DFS, and sportsbook workflows
  • API keys and service-to-service access
  • Live in-game updates are not part of the initial service

03 · Authentication

Restricted service-to-service access.

Protected endpoints use the X-API-Key request header.

X-API-Key: YOUR_API_KEY
  • 200 request succeeded
  • 401 missing or invalid key
  • 404 requested player, date, or artifact unavailable
  • 422 invalid parameter or request body
  • 503 required service configuration unavailable

05 · Projection horizons

Use the horizon that matches the decision.

The same engine can expose structural rest-of-season expectations and more responsive short-window views for today, DFS, sportsbook, and player-market workflows.

season_baselinerecency_10recency_5recency_1game_day

Pass the mode explicitly. Every horizon response keeps the structural projection visible and reports how much recency affected the adjusted result.

GET /projections/today?projection_mode=recency_5 {"as_of_date":"2026-09-23","model_version":"delquant-1.2","projection_mode":"recency_5","recency_window":5,"recency_weight":0.65,"players":[{"player_id":"203999","name":"Example Player","team":"DEN","structural_projection":{"pts":25.4,"reb":11.8,"ast":8.3},"adjusted_projection":{"pts":27.1,"reb":12.2,"ast":9.0},"confidence":{"pts":0.74,"reb":0.69,"ast":0.66},"reasons":["recent_usage_up","role_stable"]}]}
GET/projections/today?projection_mode={mode}

Use season_baseline for the structural view, recency_10, recency_5, or recency_1 for increasingly responsive views, and game_day for slate-specific context.

GET/projections/player/{player_id}/modes

Compare all horizon outputs for one player in a single response.

06 · Short-horizon services

Build decision products on top of the projection layer.

WinstAPlayer and DFSRase are downstream DelQuant service profiles for short-term decisions. They do not replace the structural or rest-of-season projection, and they do not require a separate long-term model.

Market service

WinstAPlayer

Use responsive player projections for individual-market research, market comparison, and decision workflows. WinstAPlayer should consume the structural baseline plus recency_5, recency_1, or game_day context as appropriate; market filters, edge thresholds, and staking remain downstream application logic.

Primary surfaces: /projections/today?projection_mode=recency_5, /projections/player/{player_id}/modes, and /api/game-day/projections.

DFS service

DFSRase

Use slate-specific projections and lineup context for fantasy decisions. DFSRase should consume recency_5, recency_1, or game_day outputs, then apply salary, ownership, contest, exposure, and lineup rules in its own decision layer.

Primary surfaces: /api/game-day/projections, /tools/lineup/optimize, and /tools/streaming-candidates.

Integration boundary. DelQuant owns the source projection, uncertainty, recency adjustment, and game context. WinstAPlayer owns market decisions; DFSRase owns slate and lineup decisions. Keep the source projection and the applied adjustment visible in downstream payloads.

10 · Protected feeds

More data for controlled partners.

Use this feed when the standard projection endpoints are not enough for the product you are operating.

GET/api/internal/baseline-projections

This is a permissioned, higher-granularity baseline feed. It is useful when you need a bulk player contract, category-level fields, minutes, durability, games-played history, auction value, and consistency in one scheduled response; when your product needs stable downstream fields rather than assembling several endpoints; or when you need partner-specific access and delivery controls. It is not required for ordinary projection lookups, player profiles, or single-player analysis.
