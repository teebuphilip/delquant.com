What DelQuant Is Showing

DelQuant is used here as a discovery and decision-support layer: it turns historical player production into functional archetypes, then studies how those player types fit inside winning rosters.

Why Atlanta

The Hawks provide a clean RFP case: multiple roster eras, a 60-win motion offense peak, a Trae Young conference-finals spike, and a current group that needs role-fit clarity.

What Is Protected

This demo shows curated outputs. Model internals, exact scoring logic, formulas, and weights are intentionally withheld.
