# DelQuant Pilot Guide

## A short path from interest to evidence

The pilot is designed to answer one question: does DelQuant create enough incremental value to earn a place in the customer's product or workflow?

It does not require replacing the current provider on day one. DelQuant can run alongside the incumbent so the customer can compare signal, context, usability, and integration effort before making a commercial decision.

## Phase 1: Define the test

**Timing:** 1–2 weeks

Agree on:

- the target use case;
- the projection horizon or horizons;
- the player universe and dates;
- the current source or baseline used for comparison;
- the success metrics;
- the delivery format and technical owner.

Typical starting use cases include player cards, fantasy research, market research, roster analysis, editorial content, or an internal analyst workflow.

## Phase 2: Run the comparison

**Timing:** 1–2 weeks

DelQuant provides the agreed API or sample feed. The customer tests the output in its own workflow and compares:

- projection accuracy against the agreed outcome data;
- differences from the current provider or internal model;
- confidence and uncertainty usefulness;
- historical-comp and archetype relevance;
- integration effort, response shape, and delivery reliability;
- analyst, editorial, or end-user response where applicable.

The customer should keep the player pool, dates, and scoring rules fixed during the evaluation. DelQuant will document both useful signal and misses.

## Phase 3: Decide the production shape

Choose the smallest commercial deployment that proves value:

- API integration;
- scheduled JSON or CSV feed;
- embedded player-intelligence module;
- internal research and analyst access;
- report or content license;
- broader enterprise deployment.

Commercial terms depend on coverage, access pattern, delivery cadence, users, and redistribution rights.

## What the customer receives

- scoped API or sample-feed access;
- representative response contracts and sample programs;
- projection, context, and intelligence outputs relevant to the test;
- technical onboarding support;
- a comparison readout with agreed metrics;
- a clear go/no-go recommendation.

## What DelQuant is not asking the customer to do

- abandon a current provider before testing;
- rebuild the customer product around a new data model;
- accept an unexplained black-box score;
- sign a long-term commitment before the evaluation has produced evidence.

## The close

“Give us one workflow and the same benchmark you use for everyone else. We will show where DelQuant adds value, where it does not, and what it would take to put the useful pieces into production.”
