Historical player analysis

Turn a player comparison into a product your customers can use.

DelQuant analyzes player shape across NBA history and connects the comparison to role, production, and availability. It can support analyst workflows, editorial research, player cards, and customer-facing explanations.

Not a trivia feature

A useful comparison explains what a player is doing, what historical role it resembles, and where the comparison breaks down. Availability is part of the read—not an afterthought.

Example: SGA

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander’s profile maps closely to his own 2023–24 season: primary-creator scoring, elite playmaking, and reliable free-throw production.

Integration surface

Put the model inside the product you already sell.

DelQuant is available as an API, JSON feed, batch delivery, or research/reporting layer. Your team can consume the projection contract directly or use the outputs to power a customer-facing experience.

Projection feed

Current player projections with canonical IDs, projected minutes, category outputs, uncertainty, and confidence fields.

Player intelligence

Player profiles, archetypes, historical comparisons, role context, and decision-ready analysis for internal or customer-facing workflows.

Replay and research

Dated historical projections and no-lookahead replay so your team can inspect the model at the decision date.
