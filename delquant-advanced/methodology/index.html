DELQUANT Advanced

Model Methodology

This page explains the strict-wins stack, the advanced offense/defense layer, and how VORP is used as an external benchmark.

Strict Wins FMVW Advanced Offense Advanced Defense VORP Benchmark

What the model does

1. Projection layerStart with the 26-27 player projection bundle, which carries games, minutes, scoring, shooting, usage, and pressure signals.
2. Advanced offenseScore offensive creation, pressure, efficiency, and security from the historical offensive tracking layer.
3. Advanced defenseScore defensive suppression, rim protection, pressure, possession control, and discipline from the historical defensive tracking layer.
4. Combined projectionBlend offense and defense into a single advanced projection score.
5. Wins bridgeCalibrate the advanced projection score to team wins with a linear fit from the historical league table.
6. Strict winsApply the calibrated bridge to produce one strict-wins number per player.
7. External benchmarkCompare strict wins against Basketball-Reference VORP to check whether the model lands in the same neighborhood.

Advanced offense inputs

The offense layer uses the historical offensive archive and the current projection bundle.

BucketPrimary inputsPurpose
CreationUSG%, AST, points, role signalsHow much offense the player creates

Advanced defense inputs

The defense layer uses the historical defensive tracking archive and the current projection bundle.

BucketPrimary inputsPurpose
SuppressionOpponent shot defense by distance, opponent FG%How much the player limits clean looks
Rim protectionAt-rim defense, blocks, block rateHow well the player protects the basket
PressureSteals, deflections, disruption proxiesHow much the player disrupts possessions
ControlRebounds, opponent rebounds, possession controlHow well the player ends possessions
DisciplineFouls, foul economy, mistakesHow often the player gives value back

Strict-wins calibration

Baseline6.0 wins is the starting point before the advanced score is applied.
SharesOffense and defense are weighted 55% / 45% inside the advanced projection layer.
BridgeThe team score-to-wins fit is a linear regression on the historical league table.
OutputStrict wins is the final player-level wins number used on the page.

How to read VORP

PurposeVORP is a public benchmark, not a model input.
UseIt lets you check whether DELQUANT lands in the same neighborhood as an established replacement-level framework.
Current fitThe current comparison shows moderate correlation, which is enough for a sanity check but not enough to claim equivalence.
